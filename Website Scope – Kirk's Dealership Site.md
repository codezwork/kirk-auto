# Website Scope – Kirk's Dealership Site

Oct 17, 2026 · @Mamba

## Overview

Dikshansh Raipure will design and build a mobile-friendly website for Kirk's vehicle sales business in British Columbia for a fixed price of CAD 1,000, delivered in about 4 weeks from the deposit.

The site has one job: turn visitors into phone calls, messages and financing or trade-in enquiries. It will show Kirk's current inventory, make it easy to contact him, and follow BC vehicle advertising rules (all-in total pricing, dealer name and number shown).

## Pages and features included

Six pages plus the features below are included in the CAD 1,000 price.

| Page | What it contains |
| --- | --- |
| Home | Headline, quick vehicle search, featured cars, why buy from Kirk, reviews, call and WhatsApp buttons |
| Inventory | Grid of all vehicles with filters for make, price, year, body type and mileage |
| Vehicle detail | Photo gallery, all-in price, key specs, description, "Book a test drive" and "Ask about this car" forms |
| Financing | Short pre-approval enquiry form (name, phone, email, budget, employment); no credit checks run on the site |
| Trade-in / Sell your car | Form with vehicle details and photo upload |
| About & Contact | Kirk's story, dealer name and number, location or service area, hours, map, contact form |

**Features across the site**

- Mobile-first design that loads fast on phones
- Tap-to-call, WhatsApp and SMS buttons on every page
- All enquiry forms send to Kirk's email (and optionally a Google Sheet)
- Simple admin so Kirk can add, edit, mark sold and remove vehicles himself, with up to 20 photos per car
- Price field set up as the all-in total price, with any dealer fees shown, to match BC advertising rules
- Dealer name and registration number in the footer of every page
- Basic SEO: page titles, descriptions, Google Search Console setup, sitemap
- Google Business Profile link and Google Maps embed
- Facebook pixel and Google Analytics installed for future ads
- SSL (https) and basic spam protection on forms

These are outside the CAD 1,000 price; each can be added later with its own quote.

- Online payments, deposits or a real credit application connected to a lender
- Copywriting beyond light editing of the text Kirk provides

## Timeline and milestones

The site launches about 4 weeks after the deposit, with Kirk's approval at three checkpoints.

1. **Week 0 — Kickoff.** Scope approved, deposit paid, Kirk's materials received.
2. **Week 1 — Homepage design.** Homepage mockup shared; Kirk gives one consolidated round of feedback.
3. **Week 2 — Full build.** All six pages built on a private preview link; first vehicles loaded.
4. **Week 3 — Review.** Kirk tests the site and forms on his phone; second round of changes.
5. **Week 4 — Launch.** Final payment, site goes live on Kirk's domain, 30-minute walkthrough of the admin.

If feedback or materials arrive late, the dates move by the same number of days.

Two rounds of changes are included, followed by 30 days of free fixes after launch.

- **Revisions:** 2 rounds (Week 1 on the homepage design, Week 3 on the full site). Each round is one consolidated list of changes from Kirk.
- **Extra changes:** new pages, new features or a third round are quoted before any work is done.

- **Support:** 30 days after launch to fix anything that does not work as described in this scope.

**Approval:** Kirk confirms this scope in writing (WhatsApp or email "approved" is enough) and sends the deposit to start.
