# SEO keyword map – Kirk's site

Oct 17, 2026. Built from Kirk's meeting (buyers at ~700 credit or below, newcomers, people rebuilding after credit problems, first car, cheap or lifted trucks, cars under $25k, pre-approval, delivery), Google Canada autocomplete (gl=ca), Google Trends (British Columbia), and the Mangools KWFinder results for "bad credit car loans vancouver".

## What the data says

- **Each phrase is small.** Mangools shows about 20 searches a month for "bad credit car loans vancouver" and 10 or fewer for its variants (location: Vancouver city). Google Trends has too little data in BC to chart any exact bad-credit phrase. Traffic will come from ranking for many related phrases, not one big keyword.
- **Competition is weak.** KD 9/100 (Easy). The top 7 Vancouver results are dealer finance pages with about 1 backlink each, plus a blog post on a site with DA 4 at #5. A focused page can reach page 1. The search results don't need a strong domain.
- **"Vancouver" is polluted by Vancouver, WA.** Autocomplete suggests "…vancouver wa" for most Vancouver phrases. Always write "Vancouver, BC", "Surrey, BC" or "BC" in titles.
- **Google autocompletes Surrey, Kelowna and Victoria with "bad credit", but not Langley, Abbotsford, Burnaby or Coquitlam.** For those cities, people search "used trucks Langley", "used cars Abbotsford" or "car loan Langley" instead.
- **"Near me" searches are won by the Google Business Profile (map pack), not by page text.** Examples are "bad credit car dealerships near me" and "car dealerships near me bad credit ok".
- **Canadian-only terms are a gift.** "Consumer proposal" and "newcomer" have far less US competition than "bad credit".

## Keywords to address but not promise

These get searched, but promising them breaks Kirk's honest positioning and may break advertising rules (VSA BC and the Competition Act on misleading claims). Answer them in an FAQ ("Is guaranteed approval real?") instead of putting them in a headline.

- guaranteed car approval / guaranteed car loan approval / car dealerships that approve everyone
- no credit check car loans / no credit check car dealership. Kirk does pull credit, so this phrase would be false.
- no money down / 0 down car loan bad credit. Use it only with the real conditions next to it.

## Keywords to skip

- buy here pay here (BC results are mostly Vancouver, WA, and Kirk arranges bank financing, not in-house loans)
- "in house financing" (same reason)
- bank-branded searches (RBC/TD/CIBC newcomer car loan): the banks own these.
- "car delivery BC" (people mean shipping/transport, not dealer delivery)
